Delivery process

The client sees the work. The live audience sees only the finished result.

When the platform permits it, development takes place in a private sandbox. Progress, priorities and feedback remain visible on a shared Trello board. Henk leads technical development; Ellen handles client communication and coordination.

  1. 01
    Understand

    Clarify the objective, risks, data and acceptance criteria.

  2. 02
    Build privately

    Implement and test in a controlled environment.

  3. 03
    Review together

    Share progress, receive comments and record approval.

  4. 04
    Release safely

    Deploy, verify and document the completed change.
